Why You'll Love Stick Jump

A deceptively simple arcade game that challenges your timing, patience, and precision with every single jump.

⏱️

Perfect Timing

Every jump demands precision. Hold to extend your stick, release at the right moment, and watch your stickman cross the gap. Too short? Too long? Game over.

🎯

One-Tap Controls

No complicated button combos. Just click or tap — anyone can learn in seconds, but only the best can master the perfect stick length every time.

🏆

Beat Your Best

Chase your high score across endless platforms. Each round is a new opportunity to push further, jump longer, and prove you have the sharpest reflexes around.

How to Play in 4 Steps

From first click to high-score hero — here's all you need to know.

1

Stand Ready

Your stickman stands on the edge of a platform, waiting for you to act.

2

Hold to Extend

Click and hold (or tap and hold) to grow your stick. The longer you hold, the longer it gets.

3

Release to Drop

Let go and the stick falls forward, creating a bridge to the next platform.

4

Cross & Repeat

If the stick reaches — you walk across! If not — you plummet. Keep going for a high score!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Stick Jump, answered.

Is Stick Jump free to play? +

Yes! Stick Jump is completely free to play in your browser. No downloads, no sign-ups, no hidden fees. Just open the page and start jumping.

Can I play Stick Jump on my phone? +

Absolutely. Stick Jump works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. On touchscreens, simply tap and hold to extend the stick, then release to drop it.

How do I improve my high score? +

Practice makes perfect. Focus on developing a consistent rhythm and try to gauge the distance visually before each jump. Check out our blog for in-depth strategy guides.

Does Stick Jump save my progress? +

Stick Jump is an endless arcade game — each session starts fresh. Your goal is to beat your personal best score every time you play. There's no saved progress, which keeps every round exciting.

What browser works best for Stick Jump? +

Stick Jump runs smoothly on all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, make sure your browser is up to date.
